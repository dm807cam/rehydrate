reHydrate

An offline companion for the reMarkable.

Pulls your notebooks over USB, makes proper PDFs from your handwriting, turns them into editable text, and posts them to your blog as drafts — all without sending your notes to anybody else's server.

macOS Apple Silicon Linux AppImage Windows Installer

Truly offline

reHydrate connects to your reMarkable through its USB cable. No Wi-Fi, no subscription, no servers in the middle. Your notes go from the tablet to your computer and stop there.

There's no reMarkable Connect needed and no monthly fee. The app works exactly the same with the tablet's cloud features turned off entirely.

Version history

Every time you sync, reHydrate keeps a snapshot of every notebook that changed. Decided you preferred last week's draft? It's still there — open the history view and restore. Want to see how a notebook looked a month ago? Same.

If you have more than one reMarkable, keep a separate library for each one and switch between them from the toolbar.

Beautiful PDFs

Notebooks open as proper PDFs — not blurry screenshots. Every pen, ballpoint, marker, fineliner, calligraphy stroke, and highlighter looks the way it does on the tablet. Sharp at any zoom, ready to print or share.

Handwriting to text

Click Convert to text on any notebook and reHydrate reads your handwriting and turns it into editable text. It works on English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, and most other scripts.

The first time you do this, the app downloads the recognition engine to your computer (a one-time setup that takes a moment). After that everything happens locally — no internet needed, no notes uploaded to anyone.

Publish to your blog

Once your handwriting is text, save it as a plain text file, a Markdown file, or send it straight to your Ghost or WordPress site as a draft. Polish and publish from your CMS the way you usually do — only this time the rough first pass came from your handwriting.

Privacy

reHydrate is built so your notes never leave your computer unless you click "publish" to a destination you chose. Concretely:

  • No telemetry, no analytics, no "anonymous usage statistics". The app makes no network calls on launch.
  • Sync between your tablet and your computer happens entirely over the USB cable. The reMarkable's cloud is not in the loop.
  • Handwriting recognition runs on your machine. The text never gets sent anywhere for processing.
  • Passwords for your tablet and your blog are stored in your computer's secure password store (Keychain on macOS, the equivalent on Linux and Windows). Never in plain text on disk.
  • The only servers the app ever contacts are: your tablet (over USB), the recognition-model download (once, the first time you use OCR), and your own blog (only when you click publish).
  • An automated check runs on every release to make sure nobody accidentally adds a network call somewhere they shouldn't.

reHydrate is unaffiliated with reMarkable AS.
